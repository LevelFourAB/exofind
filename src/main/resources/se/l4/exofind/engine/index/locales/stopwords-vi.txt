# Vietnamese stopwords, one per line.
#
# Vietnamese writes a space between syllables rather than between words, and
# the engine indexes the syllables. This list holds the syllables that are
# grammar on their own: conjunctions, prepositions, tense and aspect markers,
# negation, degree, pronouns, demonstratives, determiners and particles.
#
# A syllable is left out when it is as often part of a content word as it is
# grammar - `sao` is why but also star, `bao` is how much but also a case,
# `tất` is all but also a sock, and the kinship terms used as pronouns name the
# people a product is for. Dropping such a syllable would lose the word it
# belongs to from every document.
