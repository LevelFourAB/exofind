Exofind examples

Examples

  • Swedish food search

    2 606 foods from the Swedish Food Agency. Searching for sås finds gravlaxsås, because the index splits compound words.

    compound splitting · facets counted sideways of filters · range buckets · highlighting

  • Airport type-ahead

    Every airport with an IATA code, completed as you type. sto becomes Stockholm, stockhlm is forgiven, and ord finds O'Hare.

    autocomplete · typo tolerance · prefix matchers · per-field weights · distance filters and sorts

  • Cleveland Museum of Art

    30 000 objects from the open access collection. Tick a department and the wall of thumbnails changes while the department counts stay as they were.

    facets counted sideways of filters · range buckets · ordering · numbered pages against cursors · highlighting

Every page searches the node these pages were built for. The index definitions and the scripts that load them live beside each page in the repository.
